# Surah 1: Al-Fatihah (Al-Fātiĥah)
**Arabic Name:** الفاتحة | **Revelation:** Makkah | **Verses:** 7
**Translation:** Saheeh International

## Verses

1. In the name of Allāh,1 the Entirely Merciful, the Especially Merciful.2
2. [All] praise is [due] to Allāh, Lord1 of the worlds -
3. The Entirely Merciful, the Especially Merciful,
4. Sovereign of the Day of Recompense.1
5. It is You we worship and You we ask for help.
6. Guide us to the straight path -
7. The path of those upon whom You have bestowed favor, not of those who have earned [Your] anger or of those who are astray.
