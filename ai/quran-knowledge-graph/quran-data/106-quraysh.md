# Surah 106: Quraysh (Quraysh)
**Arabic Name:** قريش | **Revelation:** Makkah | **Verses:** 4
**Translation:** Saheeh International

## Verses

1. For the accustomed security of the Quraysh1 -
2. Their accustomed security [in] the caravan of winter and summer1 -
3. Let them worship the Lord of this House,1
4. Who has fed them, [saving them] from hunger and made them safe, [saving them] from fear.
