# Surah 97: Al-Qadr (Al-Qadr)
**Arabic Name:** القدر | **Revelation:** Makkah | **Verses:** 5
**Translation:** Saheeh International

## Verses

1. Indeed, We sent it [i.e., the Qur’ān] down during the Night of Decree.
2. And what can make you know what is the Night of Decree?
3. The Night of Decree is better than a thousand months.
4. The angels and the Spirit [i.e., Gabriel] descend therein by permission of their Lord for every matter.1
5. Peace it is1 until the emergence of dawn.
