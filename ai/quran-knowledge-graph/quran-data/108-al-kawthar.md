# Surah 108: Al-Kawthar (Al-Kawthar)
**Arabic Name:** الكوثر | **Revelation:** Makkah | **Verses:** 3
**Translation:** Saheeh International

## Verses

1. Indeed, We have granted you, [O Muḥammad], al-Kawthar.
2. So pray to your Lord and offer sacrifice [to Him alone].
3. Indeed, your enemy is the one cut off.1
