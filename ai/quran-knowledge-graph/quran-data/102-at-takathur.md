# Surah 102: At-Takathur (At-Takāthur)
**Arabic Name:** التكاثر | **Revelation:** Makkah | **Verses:** 8
**Translation:** Saheeh International

## Verses

1. Competition in [worldly] increase diverts you
2. Until you visit the graveyards.1
3. No! You are going to know.
4. Then, no! You are going to know.
5. No! If you only knew with knowledge of certainty...1
6. You will surely see the Hellfire.
7. Then you will surely see it with the eye of certainty.1
8. Then you will surely be asked that Day about pleasure.1
