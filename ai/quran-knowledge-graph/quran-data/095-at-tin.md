# Surah 95: At-Tin (At-Tīn)
**Arabic Name:** التين | **Revelation:** Makkah | **Verses:** 8
**Translation:** Saheeh International

## Verses

1. By the fig and the olive1
2. And [by] Mount Sinai
3. And [by] this secure city [i.e., Makkah],
4. We have certainly created man in the best of stature;1
5. Then We return him to the lowest of the low,1
6. Except for those who believe and do righteous deeds, for they will have a reward uninterrupted.
7. So what yet causes you to deny the Recompense?1
8. Is not Allāh the most just of judges?
