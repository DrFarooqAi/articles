# Surah 110: An-Nasr (An-Naşr)
**Arabic Name:** النصر | **Revelation:** Madinah | **Verses:** 3
**Translation:** Saheeh International

## Verses

1. When the victory of Allāh has come and the conquest,1
2. And you see the people entering into the religion of Allāh in multitudes,
3. Then exalt [Him] with praise of your Lord and ask forgiveness of Him. Indeed, He is ever Accepting of Repentance.1
