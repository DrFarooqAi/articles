# Ruku 250: Al-Isra (17:94–17:100)
**Surah:** Al-Isra (الإسراء) · Surah 17 · Makkah
**Juz:** 15 | **Verse range:** 17:94–17:100 | **Ruku:** 250/558
**Verse keys:** 17:94, 17:95, 17:96, 17:97, 17:98, 17:99, 17:100
**Translation:** Saheeh International

## Verses

**17:94** And what prevented the people from believing when guidance came to them except that they said, "Has Allāh sent a human messenger?"

**17:95** Say, "If there were upon the earth angels walking securely,1 We would have sent down to them from the heaven an angel [as a] messenger."

**17:96** Say, "Sufficient is Allāh as Witness between me and you. Indeed He is ever, concerning His servants, Aware and Seeing."

**17:97** And whoever Allāh guides - he is the [rightly] guided; and whoever He sends astray1 - you will never find for them protectors besides Him, and We will gather them on the Day of Resurrection [fallen] on their faces - blind, dumb and deaf. Their refuge is Hell; every time it subsides, We increase [for] them blazing fire.

**17:98** That is their recompense because they disbelieved in Our verses and said, "When we are bones and crumbled particles, will we [truly] be resurrected [in] a new creation?"

**17:99** Do they not see that Allāh, who created the heavens and earth, is [the one] Able to create the likes of them? And He has appointed for them a term, about which there is no doubt. But the wrongdoers refuse except disbelief.

**17:100** Say [to them], "If you possessed the depositories of the mercy of my Lord, then you would withhold out of fear of spending." And ever has man been stingy.
