# Ruku 521: An-Naba (78:31–78:40)
**Surah:** An-Naba (النبإ) · Surah 78 · Makkah
**Juz:** 30 | **Verse range:** 78:31–78:40 | **Ruku:** 521/558
**Verse keys:** 78:31, 78:32, 78:33, 78:34, 78:35, 78:36, 78:37, 78:38, 78:39, 78:40
**Translation:** Saheeh International

## Verses

**78:31** Indeed, for the righteous is attainment1 -

**78:32** Gardens and grapevines.

**78:33** And full-breasted [companions] of equal age.

**78:34** And a full cup.1

**78:35** No ill speech will they hear therein or any falsehood -

**78:36** [As] reward from your Lord, [a generous] gift [made due by] account,1

**78:37** [From] the Lord of the heavens and the earth and whatever is between them, the Most Merciful. They possess not from Him [authority for] speech.1

**78:38** The Day that the Spirit [i.e., Gabriel] and the angels will stand in rows, they will not speak except for one whom the Most Merciful permits, and he will say what is correct.

**78:39** That is the True [i.e., certain] Day; so he who wills may take to his Lord a [way of] return.1

**78:40** Indeed, We have warned you of an impending punishment on the Day when a man will observe what his hands have put forth1 and the disbeliever will say, "Oh, I wish that I were dust!"
