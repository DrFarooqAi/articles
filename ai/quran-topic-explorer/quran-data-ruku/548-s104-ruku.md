# Ruku 548: Al-Humazah (104:1–104:9)
**Surah:** Al-Humazah (الهمزة) · Surah 104 · Makkah
**Juz:** 30 | **Verse range:** 104:1–104:9 | **Ruku:** 548/558
**Verse keys:** 104:1, 104:2, 104:3, 104:4, 104:5, 104:6, 104:7, 104:8, 104:9
**Translation:** Saheeh International

## Verses

**104:1** Woe to every scorner and mocker

**104:2** Who collects wealth and [continuously] counts it.1

**104:3** He thinks that his wealth will make him immortal.

**104:4** No! He will surely be thrown into the Crusher.1

**104:5** And what can make you know what is the Crusher?

**104:6** It is the fire of Allāh, [eternally] fueled,

**104:7** Which mounts directed at the hearts.1

**104:8** Indeed, it [i.e., Hellfire] will be closed down upon them

**104:9** In extended columns.1
