# Ruku 438: Al-Jathiyah (45:22–45:26)
**Surah:** Al-Jathiyah (الجاثية) · Surah 45 · Makkah
**Juz:** 25 | **Verse range:** 45:22–45:26 | **Ruku:** 438/558
**Verse keys:** 45:22, 45:23, 45:24, 45:25, 45:26
**Translation:** Saheeh International

## Verses

**45:22** And Allāh created the heavens and earth in truth and so that every soul may be recompensed for what it has earned, and they will not be wronged.

**45:23** Have you seen he who has taken as his god his [own] desire, and Allāh has sent him astray due to knowledge1 and has set a seal upon his hearing and his heart and put over his vision a veil? So who will guide him after Allāh? Then will you not be reminded?

**45:24** And they say, "There is not but our worldly life; we die and live,1 and nothing destroys us except time." And they have of that no knowledge; they are only assuming.

**45:25** And when Our verses are recited to them as clear evidences, their argument is only that they say, "Bring [back] our forefathers, if you should be truthful."

**45:26** Say, "Allāh causes you to live, then causes you to die; then He will assemble you for the Day of Resurrection, about which there is no doubt, but most of the people do not know."
