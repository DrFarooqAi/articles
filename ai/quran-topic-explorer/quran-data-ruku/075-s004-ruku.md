# Ruku 75: An-Nisa (4:97–4:100)
**Surah:** An-Nisa (النساء) · Surah 4 · Madinah
**Juz:** 5 | **Verse range:** 4:97–4:100 | **Ruku:** 75/558
**Verse keys:** 4:97, 4:98, 4:99, 4:100
**Translation:** Saheeh International

## Verses

**4:97** Indeed, those whom the angels take [in death] while wronging themselves1 - [the angels] will say, "In what [condition] were you?" They will say, "We were oppressed in the land." They [the angels] will say, "Was not the earth of Allāh spacious [enough] for you to emigrate therein?" For those, their refuge is Hell - and evil it is as a destination.

**4:98** Except for the oppressed among men, women, and children who cannot devise a plan nor are they directed to a way1 -

**4:99** For those it is expected that Allāh will pardon them, and Allāh is ever Pardoning1 and Forgiving.

**4:100** And whoever emigrates for the cause of Allāh will find on the earth many [alternative] locations and abundance. And whoever leaves his home as an emigrant to Allāh and His Messenger and then death overtakes him - his reward has already become incumbent upon Allāh. And Allāh is ever Forgiving and Merciful.
