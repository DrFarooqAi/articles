# Ruku 355: Ar-Rum (30:54–30:60)
**Surah:** Ar-Rum (الروم) · Surah 30 · Makkah
**Juz:** 21 | **Verse range:** 30:54–30:60 | **Ruku:** 355/558
**Verse keys:** 30:54, 30:55, 30:56, 30:57, 30:58, 30:59, 30:60
**Translation:** Saheeh International

## Verses

**30:54** Allāh is the one who created you from weakness, then made after weakness strength, then made after strength weakness and white hair. He creates what He wills, and He is the Knowing, the Competent.

**30:55** And the Day the Hour appears the criminals will swear they had remained but an hour. Thus they were deluded.

**30:56** But those who were given knowledge and faith will say, "You remained the extent of Allāh's decree until the Day of Resurrection, and this is the Day of Resurrection, but you did not used to know."1

**30:57** So that Day, their excuse will not benefit those who wronged, nor will they be asked to appease [Allāh].

**30:58** And We have certainly presented to the people in this Qur’ān from every [kind of] example. But, [O Muḥammad], if you should bring them a sign, the disbelievers will surely say, "You [believers] are but falsifiers."

**30:59** Thus does Allāh seal the hearts of those who do not know.1

**30:60** So be patient. Indeed, the promise of Allāh is truth. And let them not disquiet you who are not certain [in faith].
