# Ruku 287: Al-Hajj (22:23–22:25)
**Surah:** Al-Hajj (الحج) · Surah 22 · Madinah
**Juz:** 17 | **Verse range:** 22:23–22:25 | **Ruku:** 287/558
**Verse keys:** 22:23, 22:24, 22:25
**Translation:** Saheeh International

## Verses

**22:23** Indeed, Allāh will admit those who believe and do righteous deeds to gardens beneath which rivers flow. They will be adorned therein with bracelets of gold and pearl, and their garments therein will be silk.

**22:24** And they had been guided [in worldly life] to good speech, and they were guided to the path of the Praiseworthy.

**22:25** Indeed, those who have disbelieved and avert [people] from the way of Allāh and [from] al-Masjid al-Ḥarām, which We made for the people - equal are the resident therein and one from outside - and [also] whoever intends [a deed] therein1 of deviation [in religion] by wrongdoing - We will make him taste of a painful punishment.
