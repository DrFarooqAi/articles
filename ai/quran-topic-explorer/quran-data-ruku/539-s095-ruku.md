# Ruku 539: At-Tin (95:1–95:8)
**Surah:** At-Tin (التين) · Surah 95 · Makkah
**Juz:** 30 | **Verse range:** 95:1–95:8 | **Ruku:** 539/558
**Verse keys:** 95:1, 95:2, 95:3, 95:4, 95:5, 95:6, 95:7, 95:8
**Translation:** Saheeh International

## Verses

**95:1** By the fig and the olive1

**95:2** And [by] Mount Sinai

**95:3** And [by] this secure city [i.e., Makkah],

**95:4** We have certainly created man in the best of stature;1

**95:5** Then We return him to the lowest of the low,1

**95:6** Except for those who believe and do righteous deeds, for they will have a reward uninterrupted.

**95:7** So what yet causes you to deny the Recompense?1

**95:8** Is not Allāh the most just of judges?
