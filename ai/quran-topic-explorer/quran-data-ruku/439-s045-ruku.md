# Ruku 439: Al-Jathiyah (45:27–45:37)
**Surah:** Al-Jathiyah (الجاثية) · Surah 45 · Makkah
**Juz:** 25 | **Verse range:** 45:27–45:37 | **Ruku:** 439/558
**Verse keys:** 45:27, 45:28, 45:29, 45:30, 45:31, 45:32, 45:33, 45:34, 45:35, 45:36, 45:37
**Translation:** Saheeh International

## Verses

**45:27** And to Allāh belongs the dominion of the heavens and the earth. And the Day the Hour appears - that Day the falsifiers will lose.

**45:28** And you will see every nation kneeling [from fear]. Every nation will be called to its record [and told], "Today you will be recompensed for what you used to do.

**45:29** This, Our record, speaks about you in truth. Indeed, We were having transcribed1 whatever you used to do."

**45:30** So as for those who believed and did righteous deeds, their Lord will admit them into His mercy. That is what is the clear attainment.

**45:31** But as for those who disbelieved, [it will be said], "Were not Our verses recited to you, but you were arrogant and became a criminal people?

**45:32** And when it was said, 'Indeed, the promise of Allāh is truth and the Hour [is coming] - no doubt about it,' you said, 'We know not what is the Hour. We assume only assumption, and we are not convinced.'"

**45:33** And the evil consequences of what they did will appear to them, and they will be enveloped by what they used to ridicule.

**45:34** And it will be said, "Today We will forget you as you forgot the meeting of this Day of yours, and your refuge is the Fire, and for you there are no helpers.

**45:35** That is because you took the verses of Allāh in ridicule, and worldly life deluded you." So that Day they will not be removed from it, nor will they be asked to appease [Allāh].

**45:36** Then, to Allāh belongs [all] praise - Lord of the heavens and Lord of the earth, Lord of the worlds.

**45:37** And to Him belongs [all] grandeur within the heavens and the earth, and He is the Exalted in Might, the Wise.
