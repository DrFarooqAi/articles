# Ruku 546: At-Takathur (102:1–102:8)
**Surah:** At-Takathur (التكاثر) · Surah 102 · Makkah
**Juz:** 30 | **Verse range:** 102:1–102:8 | **Ruku:** 546/558
**Verse keys:** 102:1, 102:2, 102:3, 102:4, 102:5, 102:6, 102:7, 102:8
**Translation:** Saheeh International

## Verses

**102:1** Competition in [worldly] increase diverts you

**102:2** Until you visit the graveyards.1

**102:3** No! You are going to know.

**102:4** Then, no! You are going to know.

**102:5** No! If you only knew with knowledge of certainty...1

**102:6** You will surely see the Hellfire.

**102:7** Then you will surely see it with the eye of certainty.1

**102:8** Then you will surely be asked that Day about pleasure.1
