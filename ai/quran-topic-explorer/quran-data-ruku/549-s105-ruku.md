# Ruku 549: Al-Fil (105:1–105:5)
**Surah:** Al-Fil (الفيل) · Surah 105 · Makkah
**Juz:** 30 | **Verse range:** 105:1–105:5 | **Ruku:** 549/558
**Verse keys:** 105:1, 105:2, 105:3, 105:4, 105:5
**Translation:** Saheeh International

## Verses

**105:1** Have you not considered, [O Muḥammad], how your Lord dealt with the companions of the elephant?1

**105:2** Did He not make their plan into misguidance?1

**105:3** And He sent against them birds in flocks,

**105:4** Striking them with stones of hard clay,

**105:5** And He made them like eaten straw.1
