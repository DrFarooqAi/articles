# Ruku 552: Al-Kawthar (108:1–108:3)
**Surah:** Al-Kawthar (الكوثر) · Surah 108 · Makkah
**Juz:** 30 | **Verse range:** 108:1–108:3 | **Ruku:** 552/558
**Verse keys:** 108:1, 108:2, 108:3
**Translation:** Saheeh International

## Verses

**108:1** Indeed, We have granted you, [O Muḥammad], al-Kawthar.

**108:2** So pray to your Lord and offer sacrifice [to Him alone].

**108:3** Indeed, your enemy is the one cut off.1
