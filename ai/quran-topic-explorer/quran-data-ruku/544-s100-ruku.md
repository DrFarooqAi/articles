# Ruku 544: Al-'Adiyat (100:1–100:11)
**Surah:** Al-'Adiyat (العاديات) · Surah 100 · Makkah
**Juz:** 30 | **Verse range:** 100:1–100:11 | **Ruku:** 544/558
**Verse keys:** 100:1, 100:2, 100:3, 100:4, 100:5, 100:6, 100:7, 100:8, 100:9, 100:10, 100:11
**Translation:** Saheeh International

## Verses

**100:1** By the racers, panting,1

**100:2** And the producers of sparks [when] striking1

**100:3** And the chargers at dawn,1

**100:4** Stirring up thereby [clouds of] dust,

**100:5** Arriving thereby in the center1 collectively,

**100:6** Indeed mankind, to his Lord, is ungrateful.

**100:7** And indeed, he is to that a witness.1

**100:8** And indeed he is, in love of wealth, intense.

**100:9** But does he not know that when the contents of the graves are scattered

**100:10** And that within the breasts is obtained,1

**100:11** Indeed, their Lord with them, that Day, is [fully] Aware.1
