# Ruku 554: An-Nasr (110:1–110:3)
**Surah:** An-Nasr (النصر) · Surah 110 · Madinah
**Juz:** 30 | **Verse range:** 110:1–110:3 | **Ruku:** 554/558
**Verse keys:** 110:1, 110:2, 110:3
**Translation:** Saheeh International

## Verses

**110:1** When the victory of Allāh has come and the conquest,1

**110:2** And you see the people entering into the religion of Allāh in multitudes,

**110:3** Then exalt [Him] with praise of your Lord and ask forgiveness of Him. Indeed, He is ever Accepting of Repentance.1
