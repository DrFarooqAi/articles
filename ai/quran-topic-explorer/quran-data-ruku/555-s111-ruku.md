# Ruku 555: Al-Masad (111:1–111:5)
**Surah:** Al-Masad (المسد) · Surah 111 · Makkah
**Juz:** 30 | **Verse range:** 111:1–111:5 | **Ruku:** 555/558
**Verse keys:** 111:1, 111:2, 111:3, 111:4, 111:5
**Translation:** Saheeh International

## Verses

**111:1** May the hands of Abū Lahab be ruined, and ruined is he.1

**111:2** His wealth will not avail him or that which he gained.

**111:3** He will [enter to] burn in a Fire of [blazing] flame

**111:4** And his wife [as well] - the carrier of firewood.1

**111:5** Around her neck is a rope of [twisted] fiber.
