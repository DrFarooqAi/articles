# Ruku 557: Al-Falaq (113:1–113:5)
**Surah:** Al-Falaq (الفلق) · Surah 113 · Makkah
**Juz:** 30 | **Verse range:** 113:1–113:5 | **Ruku:** 557/558
**Verse keys:** 113:1, 113:2, 113:3, 113:4, 113:5
**Translation:** Saheeh International

## Verses

**113:1** Say, "I seek refuge in the Lord of daybreak

**113:2** From the evil of that which He created

**113:3** And from the evil of darkness when it settles

**113:4** And from the evil of the blowers in knots1

**113:5** And from the evil of an envier when he envies."
