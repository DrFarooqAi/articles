# Ruku 264: Maryam (19:1–19:15)
**Surah:** Maryam (مريم) · Surah 19 · Makkah
**Juz:** 16 | **Verse range:** 19:1–19:15 | **Ruku:** 264/558
**Verse keys:** 19:1, 19:2, 19:3, 19:4, 19:5, 19:6, 19:7, 19:8, 19:9, 19:10, 19:11, 19:12, 19:13, 19:14, 19:15
**Translation:** Saheeh International

## Verses

**19:1** Kāf, Hā, Yā, ʿAyn, Ṣād.1

**19:2** [This is] a mention of the mercy of your Lord to His servant Zechariah

**19:3** When he called to his Lord a private call [i.e., supplication].

**19:4** He said, "My Lord, indeed my bones have weakened, and my head has filled1 with white, and never have I been in my supplication to You, my Lord, unhappy [i.e., disappointed].

**19:5** And indeed, I fear the successors1 after me, and my wife has been barren, so give me from Yourself an heir

**19:6** Who will inherit me1 and inherit from the family of Jacob. And make him, my Lord, pleasing [to You]."

**19:7** [He was told],1 "O Zechariah, indeed We give you good tidings of a boy whose name will be John. We have not assigned to any before [this] name."

**19:8** He said, "My Lord, how will I have a boy when my wife has been barren and I have reached extreme old age?"

**19:9** [An angel] said, "Thus [it will be]; your Lord says, 'It is easy for Me, for I created you before, while you were nothing.'"

**19:10** [Zechariah] said, "My Lord, make for me a sign." He said, "Your sign is that you will not speak to the people for three nights, [being] sound."1

**19:11** So he came out to his people from the prayer chamber and signaled to them to exalt [Allāh] in the morning and afternoon.

**19:12** [Allāh said], "O John, take the Scripture [i.e., adhere to it] with determination." And We gave him judgement [while yet] a boy

**19:13** And affection from Us and purity, and he was fearing of Allāh

**19:14** And dutiful to his parents, and he was not a disobedient tyrant.

**19:15** And peace be upon him the day he was born and the day he dies and the day he is raised alive.
