# Ruku 543: Az-Zalzalah (99:1–99:8)
**Surah:** Az-Zalzalah (الزلزلة) · Surah 99 · Madinah
**Juz:** 30 | **Verse range:** 99:1–99:8 | **Ruku:** 543/558
**Verse keys:** 99:1, 99:2, 99:3, 99:4, 99:5, 99:6, 99:7, 99:8
**Translation:** Saheeh International

## Verses

**99:1** When the earth is shaken with its [final] earthquake

**99:2** And the earth discharges its burdens1

**99:3** And man says,1 "What is [wrong] with it?" -

**99:4** That Day, it will report its news

**99:5** Because your Lord has inspired [i.e., commanded] it.

**99:6** That Day, the people will depart1 separated [into categories] to be shown [the result of] their deeds.

**99:7** So whoever does an atom's weight1 of good will see it,

**99:8** And whoever does an atom's weight of evil will see it.
