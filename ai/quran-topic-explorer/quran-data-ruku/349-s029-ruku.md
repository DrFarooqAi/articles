# Ruku 349: Al-'Ankabut (29:64–29:69)
**Surah:** Al-'Ankabut (العنكبوت) · Surah 29 · Makkah
**Juz:** 21 | **Verse range:** 29:64–29:69 | **Ruku:** 349/558
**Verse keys:** 29:64, 29:65, 29:66, 29:67, 29:68, 29:69
**Translation:** Saheeh International

## Verses

**29:64** And this worldly life is not but diversion and amusement. And indeed, the home of the Hereafter - that is the [eternal] life, if only they knew.

**29:65** And when they board a ship, they supplicate Allāh, sincere to Him in religion [i.e., faith and hope]. But when He delivers them to the land, at once they associate others with Him

**29:66** So that they will deny what We have granted them, and they will enjoy themselves. But they are going to know.1

**29:67** Have they not seen that We made [Makkah] a safe sanctuary, while people are being taken away1 all around them? Then in falsehood do they believe, and in the favor of Allāh they disbelieve?

**29:68** And who is more unjust than one who invents a lie about Allāh or denies the truth when it has come to him? Is there not in Hell a [sufficient] residence for the disbelievers?

**29:69** And those who strive for Us - We will surely guide them to Our ways.1 And indeed, Allāh is with the doers of good.
