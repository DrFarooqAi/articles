# Ruku 234: An-Nahl (16:77–16:83)
**Surah:** An-Nahl (النحل) · Surah 16 · Makkah
**Juz:** 14 | **Verse range:** 16:77–16:83 | **Ruku:** 234/558
**Verse keys:** 16:77, 16:78, 16:79, 16:80, 16:81, 16:82, 16:83
**Translation:** Saheeh International

## Verses

**16:77** And to Allāh belongs the unseen [aspects] of the heavens and the earth. And the command for the Hour is not but as a glance of the eye or even nearer. Indeed, Allāh is over all things competent.

**16:78** And Allāh has extracted you from the wombs of your mothers not knowing a thing, and He made for you hearing and vision and hearts [i.e., intellect] that perhaps you would be grateful.

**16:79** Do they not see the birds controlled in the atmosphere of the sky? None holds them up except Allāh. Indeed in that are signs for a people who believe.

**16:80** And Allāh has made for you from your homes a place of rest and made for you from the hides of the animals tents which you find light on your day of travel and your day of encampment; and from their wool, fur and hair is furnishing and enjoyment [i.e., provision] for a time.

**16:81** And Allāh has made for you, from that which He has created, shadows [i.e., shade] and has made for you from the mountains, shelters and has made for you garments which protect you from the heat and garments [i.e., coats of mail] which protect you from your [enemy in] battle. Thus does He complete His favor upon you that you might submit [to Him].

**16:82** But if they turn away, [O Muḥammad] - then only upon you is [responsibility for] clear notification.

**16:83** They recognize the favor of Allāh; then they deny it. And most of them are disbelievers.
