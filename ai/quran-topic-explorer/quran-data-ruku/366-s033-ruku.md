# Ruku 366: Al-Ahzab (33:28–33:34)
**Surah:** Al-Ahzab (الأحزاب) · Surah 33 · Madinah
**Juz:** 21 | **Verse range:** 33:28–33:34 | **Ruku:** 366/558
**Verse keys:** 33:28, 33:29, 33:30, 33:31, 33:32, 33:33, 33:34
**Translation:** Saheeh International

## Verses

**33:28** O Prophet, say to your wives, "If you should desire the worldly life and its adornment, then come, I will provide for you and give you a gracious release.

**33:29** But if you should desire Allāh and His Messenger and the home of the Hereafter - then indeed, Allāh has prepared for the doers of good among you a great reward."

**33:30** O wives of the Prophet, whoever of you should commit a clear immorality - for her the punishment would be doubled two fold, and ever is that, for Allāh, easy.

**33:31** And whoever of you devoutly obeys Allāh and His Messenger and does righteousness - We will give her her reward twice; and We have prepared for her a noble provision.

**33:32** O wives of the Prophet, you are not like anyone among women. If you fear Allāh, then do not be soft in speech [to men],1 lest he in whose heart is disease should covet, but speak with appropriate speech.

**33:33** And abide in your houses and do not display yourselves as [was] the display of the former times of ignorance. And establish prayer and give zakāh and obey Allāh and His Messenger. Allāh intends only to remove from you the impurity [of sin], O people of the [Prophet's] household, and to purify you with [extensive] purification.

**33:34** And remember what is recited in your houses of the verses of Allāh and wisdom.1 Indeed, Allāh is ever Subtle2 and Aware.
