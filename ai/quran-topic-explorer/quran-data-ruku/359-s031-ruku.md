# Ruku 359: Luqman (31:31–31:34)
**Surah:** Luqman (لقمان) · Surah 31 · Makkah
**Juz:** 21 | **Verse range:** 31:31–31:34 | **Ruku:** 359/558
**Verse keys:** 31:31, 31:32, 31:33, 31:34
**Translation:** Saheeh International

## Verses

**31:31** Do you not see that ships sail through the sea by the favor of Allāh that He may show you of His signs? Indeed in that are signs for everyone patient and grateful.

**31:32** And when waves come over them like canopies, they supplicate Allāh, sincere to Him in religion [i.e., faith]. But when He delivers them to the land, there are [some] of them who are moderate [in faith]. And none rejects Our signs except everyone treacherous and ungrateful.

**31:33** O mankind, fear your Lord and fear a Day when no father will avail his son, nor will a son avail his father at all. Indeed, the promise of Allāh is truth, so let not the worldly life delude you and be not deceived about Allāh by the Deceiver [i.e., Satan].

**31:34** Indeed, Allāh [alone] has knowledge of the Hour and sends down the rain and knows what is in the wombs.1 And no soul perceives what it will earn tomorrow, and no soul perceives in what land it will die. Indeed, Allāh is Knowing and Aware.
