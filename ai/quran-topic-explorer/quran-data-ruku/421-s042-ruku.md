# Ruku 421: Ash-Shuraa (42:1–42:9)
**Surah:** Ash-Shuraa (الشورى) · Surah 42 · Makkah
**Juz:** 25 | **Verse range:** 42:1–42:9 | **Ruku:** 421/558
**Verse keys:** 42:1, 42:2, 42:3, 42:4, 42:5, 42:6, 42:7, 42:8, 42:9
**Translation:** Saheeh International

## Verses

**42:1** Ḥā, Meem.

**42:2** ʿAyn, Seen, Qāf.1

**42:3** Thus has He revealed to you, [O Muḥammad], and to those before you - Allāh, the Exalted in Might, the Wise.

**42:4** To Him belongs whatever is in the heavens and whatever is in the earth, and He is the Most High, the Most Great.1

**42:5** The heavens almost break from above them,1 and the angels exalt [Allāh] with praise of their Lord and ask forgiveness for those on earth. Unquestionably, it is Allāh who is the Forgiving, the Merciful.

**42:6** And those who take as allies other than Him - Allāh is [yet] Guardian over them; and you, [O Muḥammad], are not over them a manager.

**42:7** And thus We have revealed to you an Arabic Qur’ān that you may warn the Mother of Cities [i.e., Makkah] and those around it1 and warn of the Day of Assembly, about which there is no doubt. A party will be in Paradise and a party in the Blaze.

**42:8** And if Allāh willed, He could have made them [of] one religion, but He admits whom He wills1 into His mercy. And the wrongdoers have not any protector or helper.

**42:9** Or have they taken protectors [or allies] besides Him? But Allāh - He is the Protector, and He gives life to the dead, and He is over all things competent.
