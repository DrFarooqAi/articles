# Ruku 505: Al-Ma'arij (70:36–70:44)
**Surah:** Al-Ma'arij (المعارج) · Surah 70 · Makkah
**Juz:** 29 | **Verse range:** 70:36–70:44 | **Ruku:** 505/558
**Verse keys:** 70:36, 70:37, 70:38, 70:39, 70:40, 70:41, 70:42, 70:43, 70:44
**Translation:** Saheeh International

## Verses

**70:36** So what is [the matter] with those who disbelieve, hastening [from] before you, [O Muḥammad],

**70:37** [To sit] on [your] right and [your] left in separate groups?1

**70:38** Does every person among them aspire to enter a garden of pleasure?

**70:39** No! Indeed, We have created them from that which they know.1

**70:40** So I swear by the Lord of [all] risings and settings1 that indeed We are able

**70:41** To replace them with better than them; and We are not to be outdone.

**70:42** So leave them to converse vainly and amuse themselves until they meet their Day which they are promised -

**70:43** The Day they will emerge from the graves rapidly as if they were, toward an erected idol, hastening.1

**70:44** Their eyes humbled, humiliation will cover them. That is the Day which they had been promised.
