# Ruku 226: An-Nahl (16:22–16:25)
**Surah:** An-Nahl (النحل) · Surah 16 · Makkah
**Juz:** 14 | **Verse range:** 16:22–16:25 | **Ruku:** 226/558
**Verse keys:** 16:22, 16:23, 16:24, 16:25
**Translation:** Saheeh International

## Verses

**16:22** Your god is one God. But those who do not believe in the Hereafter - their hearts are disapproving, and they are arrogant.

**16:23** Assuredly, Allāh knows what they conceal and what they declare. Indeed, He does not like the arrogant.

**16:24** And when it is said to them, "What has your Lord sent down?" they say, "Legends of the former peoples,"

**16:25** That they may bear their own burdens [i.e., sins] in full on the Day of Resurrection and some of the burdens of those whom they misguide without [i.e., by lack of] knowledge. Unquestionably, evil is that which they bear.
