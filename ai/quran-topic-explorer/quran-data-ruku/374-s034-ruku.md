# Ruku 374: Saba (34:22–34:30)
**Surah:** Saba (سبإ) · Surah 34 · Makkah
**Juz:** 22 | **Verse range:** 34:22–34:30 | **Ruku:** 374/558
**Verse keys:** 34:22, 34:23, 34:24, 34:25, 34:26, 34:27, 34:28, 34:29, 34:30
**Translation:** Saheeh International

## Verses

**34:22** Say, [O Muḥammad], "Invoke those you claim [as deities] besides Allāh." They do not possess an atom's weight [of ability] in the heavens or on the earth, and they do not have therein any partnership [with Him], nor is there for Him from among them any assistant.

**34:23** And intercession does not benefit with Him except for one whom He permits. [And those wait] until, when terror is removed from their hearts,1 they will say [to one another], "What has your Lord said?" They will say, "The truth." And He is the Most High, the Grand.

**34:24** Say, "Who provides for you from the heavens and the earth?" Say, "Allāh. And indeed, we or you are either upon guidance or in clear error."

**34:25** Say, "You will not be asked about what we committed, and we will not be asked about what you do."

**34:26** Say, "Our Lord will bring us together; then He will judge between us in truth. And He is the Knowing Judge."1

**34:27** Say, "Show me those whom you have attached to Him as partners. No! Rather, He [alone] is Allāh, the Exalted in Might, the Wise."

**34:28** And We have not sent you except comprehensively1 to mankind as a bringer of good tidings and a warner. But most of the people do not know.

**34:29** And they say, "When is this promise, if you should be truthful?"

**34:30** Say, "For you is the appointment of a Day [when] you will not remain thereafter an hour, nor will you precede [it]."
