# Ruku 550: Quraysh (106:1–106:4)
**Surah:** Quraysh (قريش) · Surah 106 · Makkah
**Juz:** 30 | **Verse range:** 106:1–106:4 | **Ruku:** 550/558
**Verse keys:** 106:1, 106:2, 106:3, 106:4
**Translation:** Saheeh International

## Verses

**106:1** For the accustomed security of the Quraysh1 -

**106:2** Their accustomed security [in] the caravan of winter and summer1 -

**106:3** Let them worship the Lord of this House,1

**106:4** Who has fed them, [saving them] from hunger and made them safe, [saving them] from fear.
