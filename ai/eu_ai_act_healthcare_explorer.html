The 4 Pillars of Compliance

While Article 5 sets the "no-go" zones, these four principles act as your compass for high-risk AI development in medicine. Compliance is a journey of continuous validation.

Transparency

Users must always be aware when they are interacting with an AI system. No hidden nudges.

Consent

Data must be sourced ethically and used only within the scope of patient authorization.

Necessity

Practices must have a clear, evidence-based clinical purpose validated by professionals.

Context

Data usage must stay strictly within the original clinical context it was collected for.
