Executive Summary

AI is not a project. It's a new operating capability for the whole organization.

Buying AI tools is the easy part. The hospitals that win will be the ones whose people can use AI safely, confidently, and in their own workflow. This plan turns the entire workforce — clinical, operational, technical, and leadership — into the engine of an AI‑native organization, sequenced so adoption is measured in outcomes, not attendance.

01

One vision

Every employee AI‑aware; every department AI‑enabled; the patient always at the centre.

02

Five tracks

Role‑based learning: a surgeon, a clerk and a board member each get what they actually need.

03

Safety first

A mandatory PDPL‑aligned baseline for all, before any tool touches patient data.

04

Measured

Adoption, competence and outcomes, never seat‑time. Governed, refreshed, sustained.

Vision · Mission · Objectives

Where we are going — and why it matters

Our Vision

To become the world's first fully AI‑native hospital — where every employee, in every department, uses AI safely and confidently as a natural part of how they work.

Our Mission

To equip every member of our workforce with the literacy, tools and trust to apply AI responsibly in their role — improving patient care, safety, and efficiency.

1

100% AI literacy. Every employee completes the universal safety baseline.

2

Role competence. Every role demonstrates AI skills specific to its work.

3

Safe by default. Full PDPL & SFDA‑aligned use of patient data.

4

A champion everywhere. A trained AI champion in every department.

5

Outcomes, not attendance. Adoption and impact are the measure of success.

6

A culture of augmentation. AI gives our people time back — it never replaces them.

The Case for Change

The technology is racing ahead. Our people must lead it.

THE SHIFT

AI is arriving everywhere

Coding, imaging, operations, triage, documentation — AI is entering every clinical and administrative process, whether we plan for it or not.

THE RISK

Tools without readiness fail

The best AI in the world sits unused if staff don't trust it, understand it, or know how to use it safely. The failure is human, not technical.

THE MANDATE

Aligned with Vision 2030

A digitally skilled, AI‑ready national workforce is a Kingdom priority. We turn that mandate into a structured, deliverable program.

Budget & Resourcing

Fund the people side deliberately

Ring‑fence a fixed share of the overall AI program budget for enablement — commonly 15–20%. It costs far less than the software and decides whether the software ever pays off.

Where the enablement budget goes

Indicative allocation across the program.

The principle

Software is a fraction of the value if no one uses it well. Enablement — content, platforms, champion time, facilitation and assessment — is the multiplier that converts spend into outcomes. Underfunding it is the most common and most expensive mistake in hospital AI.
